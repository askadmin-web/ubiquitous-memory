Role · Motion
Motion
AI Deployment Strategist · Product · Remote, Canada & United States
Creative analytics platform for marketing teams, now building Runneth, an AI operating system for marketers. The AI Deployment Strategist owns the relationship with a focused group of top customers, builds what each one needs with Runneth — custom taxonomies, reporting, data connections, and workflows — and brings what could work for every customer back to Product.
What You’ll Be Doing
Own the relationship with a focused group of top customers — talking with them to understand their goals, workflows, and what’s getting in the way
Turn what you hear into custom use cases — from custom taxonomies and reporting to new data connections and workflows not yet built
Prototype fast and scrappy to get customers a working solution, then hand off to Engineering when it needs to be built properly
Bring what you learn back to the team — flag which custom builds could become use cases for every customer and feed customer insight into the Product roadmap
Partner closely with Product and Engineering to unblock technical issues, and with CS to understand what customers need next
Give customers honest, respectful guidance — including telling them when the fix is in their strategy rather than the product
Be one of the first to try every new feature and figure out how customers can get the most out of it
Support the internal marketing team (content, creative strategy, growth) and influencer partners on their use cases
Our Ideal Fit Brings
Strong relationship skills — build trust quickly, ask the right questions to find what people actually need, and own the relationship from first call to working solution
A builder’s mindset — take something messy and turn it into a system that works better; figure out someone’s problem and own the solution
Hands-on with AI — you’ve built your own workflows, agents, or automations, and you’re comfortable talking with engineers
High agency and curiosity — an early adopter who will talk to anyone to get the job done, engineers one minute and marketers the next
Love for ambiguity — no two days look the same and priorities shift fast; impact matters more than title
Organized — juggle several customers and builds at once without dropping anything
Bonus Points
Experience in DTC, ecommerce, paid social, or creative strategy
A background in customer success or implementation with real technical depth, on the agency side, or in managing influencer and creator relationships
You’ve founded something or built your own product and loved talking to the people using it
