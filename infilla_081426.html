Implementation Lead · Remote, East Coast / mid-Atlantic
GovTech platform modernizing permitting and entitlements for municipal planning and building departments. The first dedicated implementation hire, reporting to the CEO: own each jurisdiction from kickoff through go-live and adoption, configure the product to how each department actually works, and build the implementation system — runbooks, configuration standards, migration checklists, go-live criteria — from scratch. Twelve people. Customers include San Francisco, New York City, Sonoma County, and the State of Delaware.
What You'll Do
Turn ambiguous engagements into a plan — walk into undefined engagements and create the scope, sequence, priorities, owners and definitions of done
Own outcomes and operate with intensity — full accountability for each customer from kickoff through go-live and long-term adoption
Win trust inside government — navigate diverse stakeholders with different definitions of success and build champions who carry the work when you're not in the room — partial: institutional, not government
Understand users deeply — get into the weeds of ordinances, workflows, data, and politics until you understand their operation better than they do — partial: no ordinances
Configure the product to reality — permit types, review workflows, routing, permissions, fee logic, and reporting — partial: configuration craft, not permit domain
Manage risk — anticipate blockers before they surface, track what success means for each customer, and never let a slipping date catch anyone off guard
Drive measurable impact — turn understanding into outcomes: faster reviews, higher adoption, real results
Build the implementation system from scratch — runbooks, configuration standards, migration checklists, and go-live criteria
First 90 Days and Beyond
Month 1 — Learn: shadow planners, examiners, and staff; go deep on configuration surface, data model, and integration points
Month 2 — Own: own a live implementation fully; lead training sessions onsite and remote; start building runbooks and templates
Month 3 — Lead: fully own multiple concurrent implementations; trusted advisor to stakeholders; a customer through go-live with a clean handoff to long-term support — thin: no handoff bullet on this resume
Year one: a repeatable implementation program that a second and third hire can run with faster
What's Hard About This Role
Every municipality is different — workflows, ordinances, legacy systems, and politics; create repeatable playbooks that accommodate the variation — partial: institutional variation
The problems are ambiguous — defining and solving the real problem means deeply understanding people, workflows, organizational politics, and technology
Slow-moving domain, urgent company — across a big product surface: intake, review, approvals, inspections, reporting, compliance — thin: no domain surface

3+ years delivering outcomes successfully as a forward deployed engineer, solutions engineer, implementation lead, technical program manager, or consultant
