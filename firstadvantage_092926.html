The senior, customer-facing seat between customer requirements and successful onboarding: owning complex enterprise implementations from discovery through go-live, translating technical and operational requirements into practical solutions, and accelerating time to revenue without compromising quality. 100% remote, with travel up to 25%. Lead complex enterprise implementations across the First Advantage product portfolio — from discovery and solution design through configuration, testing, go-live, stabilization, and transition to Customer Success
Turn customer business and technical requirements into scalable, supportable solutions — lead discovery, explain trade-offs and constraints, and guide customers toward best-practice decisions
Build and govern customer-centric implementation plans — clear scope, milestones, owners, dependencies, risks, decisions, and success measures
Negotiate scope, priorities, timelines, responsibilities, and solution choices with customers and internal stakeholders while maintaining trusted relationships

Drive progress toward go-live and first revenue — proactively managing scope creep, delivery risk, customer dependencies, and recovery plans
Mentor peers — sharing expertise, providing practical guidance, and supporting their growth in implementation leadership, customer engagement, and problem-solving
Use lessons learned and customer insights to improve implementation playbooks, reusable solutions, and delivery processes, while sharing expertise with peers
How You’ll Work
High emotional intelligence — listen actively, adapt your communication, and navigate challenging discussions while maintaining trust
Collaborative influence — build alignment without relying on authority and move teams toward clear decisions and shared accountability
Business acumen — connect implementation choices to customer outcomes, operational impact, revenue, risk, and scalability
Creative curiosity — ask incisive questions, explore root causes, and design practical solutions that balance customer needs with delivery standards
Learning agility — experiment thoughtfully, learn from setbacks, and adjust quickly without losing momentum
What Success Looks Like
Customers understand the implementation path, decisions required, and responsibilities needed to adopt the agreed solution
Projects are tightly scoped and actively governed, with transparent ownership, risks, decisions, and next steps
Solutions are technically feasible, commercially sound, scalable, and aligned with best practice
Cross-functional teams align early, blockers are resolved quickly, and avoidable rework and customization are reduced
Implementations reach go-live and first revenue with speed, predictability, and customer confidence

Ability to translate integrations, APIs, data exchange, configuration, and ATS/HRIS concepts into clear business implications and actionable recommendations — coding experience is not required
