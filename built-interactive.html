Illuma Engineer · Technical Services Division · On-site (various client locations)
Full-lifecycle management of facility lighting infrastructure — acquisition, testing, installation, monitoring, storage, and environmentally responsible disposal of illumination components. The role rewards technical precision, supplier coordination, and a results-driven approach to lumen performance across multiple sites. Salary $58,000–$72,000.
Key Responsibilities
Assess lighting needs and select bulbs/fixtures matching specific wattage, lumen, base, and size requirements
Procure illumination components via approved commercial channels, aligning with MegaHandy's standards
Remove and replace lighting elements safely and efficiently, ensuring code compliance
Test lighting systems for performance using standard diagnostic tools
Maintain accurate inventory and employ proper storage
Oversee recycling and disposal of retired or defective bulbs per environmental regulations
Document process improvements for lighting service and energy efficiency
Collaborate across departments to address changing illumination needs
Required Qualifications
Associate's degree or higher in Electrical Technology, Facilities Engineering, Applied Mechanics, or related field
At least 2 years' direct experience in lighting maintenance, facility operations, or technical fieldwork
Solid understanding of electrical safety codes, fixtures, and energy classifications
Demonstrated organizational skills, including inventory tracking
Ability to work at heights, lift up to 40 lbs, and use maintenance equipment
Skill in managing routine/urgent lighting issues calmly and precisely
Preferred Qualifications
Lighting Efficiency or Facilities Maintenance certification (e.g., CLMC, LEED)
Familiarity with smart lighting systems and sustainable tech — partial: sustainability only
Experience in commercial, institutional, or industrial settings
