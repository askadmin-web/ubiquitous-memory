Role · Fulcrum
Fulcrum
Launch Manager · Launch · Remote, United States
Manufacturing operating system for small and medium manufacturers, rebuilding itself as an AI-native company. The launch team is the implementation — no resellers — owning each customer from discovery through adoption, and building its own solutions with AI-assisted tools rather than queuing every technical problem for Engineering. Salary $85,000–$135,000 + equity.
Own Customer Launches
Lead customers through the full implementation process — from discovery through successful launch and adoption
Own project timelines, milestones, deliverables, and customer communication — across multiple launches
Develop a deep understanding of each customer — manufacturing operations, business goals, and existing workflows
Translate complex operational requirements — into practical solutions using Fulcrum
Anticipate risks, dependencies, and obstacles — proactively developing solutions to keep launches moving
Help customers navigate operational change — build confidence in new processes, and successfully adopt Fulcrum
Understand the Business Behind the Problem
Ask thoughtful questions — to uncover what customers actually need, even when they have difficulty articulating it
Look beyond a customer’s initial request — to understand the underlying business problem
Understand how departments, stakeholders, and processes influence one another
Navigate competing priorities — internal dynamics, and differing perspectives within customer organizations
Connect implementation decisions to broader business goals — balancing customer needs, technical capabilities, and practical constraints
Continuously expand your understanding of manufacturing operations — and identify opportunities customers may not recognize themselves
Build and Solve with AI
Independently use AI-assisted development tools — to build functional solutions, automate workflows, and solve customer problems
Create or adapt tools, troubleshoot integrations, transform data — and improve operational workflows using AI-assisted approaches
Experiment with new technologies and approaches — rather than automatically relying on Engineering for every technical challenge
Evaluate the quality, reliability, and practical usefulness of solutions — before implementing them
Recognize when deeper technical expertise is needed — and collaborate with Engineering when appropriate
Improve internal launch processes — through automation, better tooling, and repeatable solutions
Communicate, Collaborate, and Drive Outcomes
Build trusted customer relationships — through clear communication, thoughtful guidance, and consistent follow-through
Explain complex technical and operational concepts — in ways different audiences can understand
Set realistic expectations, communicate tradeoffs — and confidently navigate difficult conversations
Collaborate with Sales, Product, Engineering, Support, and Account Management — to deliver successful customer outcomes
Bring recommendations and potential solutions — when raising problems rather than simply escalating issues
Share customer insights and recurring challenges — to improve Fulcrum’s products and implementation processes
You Also Bring
Experience in customer implementation, technical consulting, solutions engineering, project management, or another role involving complex customer problems
Strong ability to understand business processes, map workflows, and translate operational needs into practical solutions
Demonstrated ability to independently use AI-assisted tools to build functional solutions, automate workflows, or solve technical problems
Experience managing complex projects involving multiple stakeholders and competing priorities
Strong technical aptitude, analytical skills, and comfort learning new software and systems
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to manage customer relationships independently
Experience with APIs, integrations, data migration, or workflow automation is a plus
Experience with manufacturing operations, ERP systems, or other complex operational software is a plus
