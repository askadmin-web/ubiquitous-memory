CertifyOS
Implementation Leader · Growth · Remote US
CertifyOS automates provider licensing, enrollment, credentialing, and network monitoring through an API-first platform connected to hundreds of primary data sources. The Implementation Leader onboards new client partners and expanding clients across all products and services, running multiple implementations from contract execution to go-live.
About the Role
Expand existing clients — oversee implementation and onboarding for existing CertifyOS clients seeking to expand their scope of services
Responsibilities
Lead implementation and onboarding — for new client partners, across all products and services, across multiple implementations simultaneously
Work cross-functionally — with key stakeholders from multiple teams
Support clients through implementation — as they complete standard implementation tasks and learn to use the CertifyOS platform
Develop deep domain understanding — of provider licensing and credentialing, and provider data management
Maintain a seamless client experience — from contract execution to go-live
Required
5+ years of professional experience overall
3+ years of experience in software implementation, project management, or management consulting
Excellent organizational skills and a strong detail-oriented mindset
Outstanding presentation, reporting, and communication skills, with the ability to tailor a message or training to a variety of audiences
Ability to work independently and manage multiple tasks simultaneously
Ability to interact and collaborate with product, engineering, and operations teams, internally and externally
Preferred
Strong understanding of provider licensing and credentialing, and the provider data management universe
Proficiency with systems, spreadsheets, databases, and applications (Google Suite, Salesforce, Jira, Confluence, Monday.com)
