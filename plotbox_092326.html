What You’ll Do
Own the end-to-end implementation lifecycle, from project planning through go-live

Confirm project scope & requirements to set clear expectations from day one, managing scope change requests throughout implementation
Serve as the primary point of contact for clients during onboarding and implementation
Facilitate regular check-ins to track progress, surface risks, and keep projects moving forward
Communicate status updates clearly to both internal stakeholders and client leadership
Solution Delivery & Best Practice Guidance
Guide clients through data migration, workflow optimization, system configuration, and integrations

Deliver training and support early adoption of the platform
Ensure clients are fully prepared for a seamless transition into long-term support

Contribute to continuous improvement of implementation processes, tools, and best practices

Desirable
